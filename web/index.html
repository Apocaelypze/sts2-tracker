💍 Relic Tier Analytics & Acquisition Breakdown
💡
Understanding Relic Metrics: Carried (Total) represents the total climbs you survived with the relic in your inventory. Chests & Drops tracks normal drops from chests and combat/elite victories. Boss Rewards tracks Act Boss relics offered and picked. Merchant Shop tracks relics purposefully purchased with gold. Events & Ancients tracks Ancient offerings (Neow, Pael, Tezcatara) and mystery events.

🧪 Potions Meta & Combat Utility
💡
Understanding Potion Metrics: Times Used counts emergency sips and strategic plays in combat. Combat Drops (Looted) tracks potions looted after monster/elite victories vs passed when your potion belt was full. Merchant Shop tracks potions purposefully bought with gold. Events & Rewards tracks potions gifted in mystery events. Win Rate tracks victories of climbs where this potion was carried or consumed.
Sort By:
Potion ↕ Times Used ↕ Combat Drops ↕ Merchant Shop ↕ Events & Rewards ↕ Win Rate ↕ Potion Effect
